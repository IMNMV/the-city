HOW TO WATCH

Play, pause
space, or the button
One day
left and right arrow keys; N and P jump to the next and previous marked day
Jump
click the timeline; the marks are deaths (red), trials (violet), laws and taxes (gold), elections (teal), births (green), houses (blue)
Camera
in 3D: drag to turn round, Shift-drag (or the middle button) to move, scroll to zoom down to street level, Q and E to turn, double-click to see the whole town; following someone puts the camera behind their shoulder. On the flat map: drag to pan, scroll to zoom. "Auto cam" (F) frames the day's action or follows your resident, and turns off when you move the camera yourself; G switches between 3D and the flat map
Follow someone
click them on the map or in the cast; click again (or press C) for their card; right-click anyone or any building for a menu of options, including a link to their card
Voices
V toggles; the narrator reads the news, each resident has a voice; "Lines/day" caps how many resident lines are read; "Voices…" lets you recast anyone and set the speed, remembered in this browser
Sound, music
S and M
Theater
T hides the side panels so the town fills the window
World map
W (or "Map") shows the whole town with every place named and everyone's dot; click a dot to follow, a building to look at it
Snapshot
saves a picture of the town as it is now
Record
R records the town with its sound as a video file, up to a minute; press again to stop and save
Panels
keys 1 to 6 open Feed, Confessionals, Court, Town Hall, Stats, Hiscores
Search
/ jumps to the search box under the panel tabs; it finds anything said, written on the board, messaged, told the court, proposed, given as a juror's reason or thought, across the whole episode; click a hit to go to its day
Quiet
a link with -quiet starts with voices, sound and music all off
Binge
a link ending in -binge rolls on to the next episode from the end card (the index has a "Binge the season" button that plays the key days of every episode back to back)
CSV
Stats has "Save these series as CSV" (every day's counts, store, tax, laws, talk, gifts, deals, town mood and where everyone is); Hiscores has "Save this board as CSV"
Data
the season folder has CSVs of everything (laws, proposals, trials, deaths, elections, residents, every day's counts, dealings) and every episode as text; see ABOUT.txt and COLUMNS.txt next to index.html, or data.zip for all of it
Text
"Save the episode as text" (end card, or the last entry of Moments) writes every day's news and everything said to a .txt file
Pace
Blitz skips the cut-away scenes
Highlights
plays only the days with a mark on the timeline and skips the quiet ones
On the map
a red dot: no food today; a bar over the head: hurt; a crown: the mayor; bars: in jail or held; a hat: a resident who took a title; a tool in hand: at work; a gravestone: where someone died; red numbers: hunger damage; flying food and coins: gifts, trades and wages
Seasons
the episode is one year: spring, summer, autumn, winter; days pass with a slow sun and moon

WORLD MAP

Everyone's place today. Click a dot to follow them; click a building to go there.
