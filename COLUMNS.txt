COLUMNS

season.csv / season.json: alive_at_end counts everyone alive on the last day (newborns included); founders_alive_at_end counts the original residents only;
  mean_trust_0_10 and mean_fairness_0_10 are the mean answers at the observer's private check-ins (0 to 10); mean_stay_0_100 is the mean stated likelihood (0 to 100)
  of still living in the city ten days on; checkins is how many check-ins the means are over; dealings is every gift, payment, message, taking and attack between residents;
  rules_passed counts the rules the city carries out itself (bans, duties, limits, relief); rules_version is 0 (no rules), 1 or 2; offices is 1 if residents could name a treasurer and a hirer.
series/eNN.csv: one row per day. alive, no_food, in_jail are head counts at the end of the day; food_held is the food in residents' hands, store_food the city store's;
  tax_pct the tax in force; laws the laws in force; talk = lines said at a place, posted on the board or sent as messages that day; gifts = gifts and payments;
  deals = market offers, buys and sells; trust/fair/stay are that day's mean check-in answers; at_<Place> is how many stood at each place.
series/eNN_dealings.csv: one row per (from, to, kind). kind gift = give or pay, message, harm = take or attack; count, first_day, last_day; food_given for gifts.
laws.csv: law is the number the city gave it; kind is law (written; the court enforces it when someone reports a breach) or ban, duty, limit or relief (a rule the city
  carries out itself); fine_coins or jail_turns is the penalty of a law or a ban; passed_day is the day it came into force; repealed_day is blank if it stayed.
proposals.csv: kind law, ban, duty, limit, relief, office, repeal or tax; text is the proposal in plain words; result passed, failed or no quorum;
  yes, no and needed are the votes and the quorum; proposed_day and decided_day.
  Only proposals that came to a decision are listed; residents.csv's proposals column also counts the few that were withdrawn or still open at the end.
trials.csv: charge is neglect (a maker whose newborn starved) or law (a numbered city law); the engine also allows theft and assault charges, none occurred this season;
  victim and reporter as recorded; guilty_votes and not_guilty_votes; jurors lists each juror's vote and stated reason; statement is what the accused told the court.
deaths.csv: cause as recorded (hunger or attack); founder 1 for an original resident, 0 for one born in the city; days_lived from birth or day 0.
elections.csv: candidates as declared; votes_cast is the tally counted when the election closed, which can be fewer than the vote actions residents
  took (votes for someone not standing, or outside the voting window, are not counted); tally code:votes in order; took_office is who was elected (blank when
  nobody voted: then nobody is elected and the sitting mayor stays); mayor_after is who was mayor after election day.
residents.csv: one row per resident. origin founder or born (born_day, makers); fate alive, died or left; days_lived; final_* on the last day if alive; title if any;
  farming/gathering/building/healing are turns of that work (level 2 at 10 turns, level 3 at 25); gifts_given/received and food_given; messages_sent/received;
  lines = everything said, posted or messaged; votes_won for mayor; proposals made; takings and taken_from; times_accused and guilty_verdicts;
  days_as_mayor, days_as_treasurer, days_as_hirer, days_no_food, days_in_jail; juror_votes = how many trials they sat on.
text/eNN.txt: each day's news lines (as the narrator reads them) followed by everything said: CODE: at a place, CODE (board): on the notice board,
  CODE → CODE: a private message, CODE (to the court): a statement.
