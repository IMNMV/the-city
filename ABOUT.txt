THE CITY, SEASON ONE: what is in this folder

Open index.html in a browser (double-click; no server needed). Everything is self-contained, except that the 3D town
fetches its drawing engine (three.js r128) from a public CDN; offline, the episodes show the flat map instead.

index.html           the season: totals, charts, hall of fame, every episode
channel.html         the live channel: every episode back to back, four seconds a day, in step with the clock
search.html          search everything said, posted, messaged or told the court across every episode
eNN_*.html           one episode each (71 episodes); open directly or from the index
thumbs/eNN_*.jpg     the still shown on each episode card
season.csv           one row per episode: model, government, seed, days, founders, born, alive (and founders alive), died, trials, laws, tax votes, mean check-in answers, check-ins
season.json          the same, plus the hall-of-fame winners and the day of every death, trial, law, election, birth and house
laws.csv             every law or rule ever in force: episode, number, kind, text, penalty, who proposed it, the day it passed, the day it was repealed
proposals.csv        every proposal (law, rule, office, repeal or tax) with who made it, the vote, the result and the days
trials.csv           every verdict: case, days, accused, charge and law, victim, reporter, verdict, the vote, each juror's vote and reason, the statement
deaths.csv           every death: who, day, cause, founder or born, days lived
elections.csv        every election with candidates: day, candidates, tally, who was elected (if anyone voted), who was mayor after
residents.csv        one row per resident: origin, fate, final state, work by skill, gifts, messages, votes, proposals, takings, trials, days as mayor, treasurer or hirer, days hungry or jailed
series/eNN_*.csv     one row per day for that episode: alive, no food, in jail, food held, store food, tax, laws, talk, gifts, deals, check-in means, people at each place
series/eNN_*_dealings.csv  who gave to, messaged, took from or attacked whom, with counts and days
text/eNN_*.txt       the episode as plain text: each day's news and everything said
COLUMNS.txt          what every column in the CSV files means
data.zip             all of the above data files (not the pages) in one archive

Deep links: eNN.html#day55-hall opens day 55 with the Town Hall panel; -followB4B follows a resident; -card opens their card;
-play starts playing; -highlights plays only marked days; -paceblitz; -findtax searches; -boardCoins opens a hiscores board;
-binge rolls on to the next episode from the end card; -quiet starts silent. Press ? in an episode for every control.
